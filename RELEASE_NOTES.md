First version.

- Samsung app: forwards your notifications to the Apple Watch over Bluetooth, following the phone's own rules (what makes a sound on the phone buzzes the watch, Do Not Disturb is respected, ongoing ones are skipped). Untick apps to mute them.
- Watch app (watchOS 6, Series 2): clock screen with phone link status and phone battery, notification list, dismiss on phone, find my phone.
- Keeps running with the screen off by holding a workout session, so notifications still buzz your wrist.
